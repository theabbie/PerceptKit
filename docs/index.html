PerceptKit

A transformer library compiled to WebAssembly — every model below is generated, trained, and evaluated live in your browser. No pretrained weights, no server. source

Digit classifier

Synthetic 8×10 font digits with random shifts and salt noise are generated in your browser; a tiny transformer (2 layers, d=16, ~9k params) is trained with the dense head to output one-hot digits per token. Then draw your own digit below.

Text-overlap scorer

Documents are rendered as line-art images; the corruption generator shifts one line into another (overlap). Trained by pairwise ranking — mild vs severe corruption of the same base doc — because absolute severity has irreducible label ambiguity (see README). Higher score = cleaner layout.

Not trained yet.

Colorizer

Synthetic scenes (sky gradient, sun, hills, water) are generated in the browser. The model sees only Rec.601 grayscale, patchified, and the dense head learns to predict the full RGB patch per token — i.e. it must invent color from luminance + context.

Not trained yet.
